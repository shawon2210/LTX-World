Model Architecture

Built for physical understanding

LTX goes beyond next-token video prediction. It learns spatial geometry, object permanence, lighting reflection, and dynamic material physics.

◆

3D World Consistency

Maintains rigorous multi-view depth and object permanence across complex cinematic camera movements with zero temporal jitter.

• Full Camera Trajectory Tracking
◘

Physical Simulation

Understands fluid flow, cloth dynamics, volumetric shadows, and realistic gravitational collision without requiring a CGI physics engine.

• Real-time Material Physics
◐

Zero-Shot Look Re-target

Seamlessly branch lighting conditions, character cast, clothing colorways, and background architectural scenes with complete identity preservation.

• Independent Branch Conditioning
⚡

Sub-Second Inference
